Privacy Policy

This policy describes how information is handled when you use the UnicLife Key Cabinet mobile app and related services.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

UnicLife Key Cabinet is a key management service for authorized users. The app processes more than a login email because cabinet administration, borrowing records, security and support require additional information.
Who is responsibleInformationPurposesSharingInternational transfersRetentionYour rightsContact

1. Who is responsible

This policy applies to the UnicLife Key Cabinet app and its related service. The service provider identified in your order form or service agreement is responsible for its account and service operations. An organization using the service may separately decide which borrower, employee, key and access details its administrators enter; for that information, the organization may be the controller and UnicLife may process it on its instructions.

For privacy questions or to identify the responsible organization for your account, email ytwl1013@gmail.com. If your request concerns data entered by your organization, we may coordinate with its authorized administrator.

2. Information we process

  • Account and profile: name, email, chosen language, password hash, authentication and session records, login activity, consent records, and optional notification token.
  • Cabinets and administration: cabinet model and identifier, name, location entered by an administrator, status, slot configuration, membership and invitations.
  • Keys and loans: key labels and notes, borrower names and optional contact details entered by an administrator, check-out and return dates, expected return dates and loan status.
  • Security and activity: operator, action, object, time, result and error code for relevant operations; limited app, device, network and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Support: messages and any information you submit in a support request. A submitted image may be included in the support email; the service stores the message and image count, while the image file is used for that delivery rather than kept in the support database.

Information comes from you, your organization and its administrators, connected cabinets, and your use of the service. Camera access is used to scan supported QR codes; camera frames are not intentionally saved or uploaded. An optional profile photo remains on your device and is not uploaded to UnicLife. The app does not request precise location, contacts, payment card details, government IDs, or advertising identifiers.

3. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; authenticate users; connect and manage cabinets; administer keys, borrowers and loans; display status and send service notifications; keep activity and audit records; answer support requests; prevent misuse; and meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

For people in the EEA or UK, the legal basis depends on the processing: performance of a contract, legitimate interests in providing and securing the service, legal obligations, or consent where required. Where consent is the basis, it can be withdrawn for future processing. A withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

4. Who may receive information

Relevant information may be visible to authorized administrators of the same cabinet or customer organization. We may use service providers for hosting, storage, email, push notifications, security and support. We may disclose information to professional advisers, a business successor, or public authorities when lawfully required. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and the app has no third-party advertising.

Providers that process personal information on our behalf are expected to protect it under appropriate contractual and security safeguards. Their own privacy notices may also apply to services such as email or mobile push delivery.

5. Storage and international transfers

Production API, database, logs and backup systems are intended to be hosted in the United States. If information from the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred to the United States or another country, we use a lawful transfer mechanism and additional safeguards where required. You may request information about the mechanism applicable to your information at the contact address below.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account and operational information while it is needed to provide the service. Afterward, information is kept only where needed for security, legal requirements, disputes or legitimate business purposes. The exact period depends on the data and its purpose. You can ask us about the period or criteria relevant to your information.

Account deletion disables access, revokes sessions, removes cabinet memberships and anonymizes the account email and name. Some historical loan references, cabinet events, support records and audit records may remain where needed for accountability, security, legal obligations or the rights of other organization members. An account that is still a cabinet Super Admin must first transfer or remove that responsibility; support can help begin this process. See Delete Account for both the in-app and web request routes.

7. Security

We use access controls, authentication, encrypted transport, limited permissions and audit logging to protect information. No system is completely secure. Please keep your account credentials and physical cabinet access secure, and report suspected misuse to support.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of your information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. EEA and UK users may also complain to their local data protection authority. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where law permits or requires it. We do not use solely automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects.

Residents of California and other US states may have additional rights under applicable state law, including rights to know, correct, delete or obtain a portable copy, and rights relating to the sale or targeted-advertising sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights. See Privacy Choices to make a request.

9. Children

The service is intended for authorized business and organizational users and is not directed to children under 16. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us so we can review it.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. Where required, we will give notice before material changes take effect. For any privacy inquiry, rights request or question about the responsible organization, email ytwl1013@gmail.com. You can also use the in-app support feature. Do not send passwords, cabinet access codes or identity documents in your first message.
