How UnicLife Key Cabinet handles personal information.

Who is responsible

This policy covers the UnicLife Key Cabinet app and service. The UnicLife provider named in the applicable order or service agreement manages account and service information. A customer organisation may separately control the borrower, employee, key and access information its administrators enter. Email ytwl1013@gmail.com to identify the provider responsible for your account.

Information and sources

We process account names, email addresses, language, password hashes, sessions, login and consent records and optional notification tokens; cabinet identifiers, locations, members and configuration; key labels, borrower names and optional contact details, loans and return dates; activity, audit and limited security diagnostics; and support messages and submitted attachments. Information comes from you, your organisation, connected cabinets and app use. QR-scanning camera frames are not intentionally stored or uploaded. An optional profile photo stays on your device.

Purposes and legal bases

We use information to authenticate and protect accounts, operate cabinets, manage keys and loans, send service alerts, keep audit and security records, respond to support, comply with law and resolve disputes. In the EEA and UK, the basis may be contract, legitimate interests, legal obligation or consent where required. Consent may be withdrawn for future processing.

Recipients, security and transfers

Authorised customer administrators may see relevant records. Providers may handle hosting, storage, email, push notifications, security and support. We use access controls, authentication, encrypted transport and audit logs. We do not sell data or share it for targeted advertising. Production systems and backups are intended to be in the United States. EEA, UK or Swiss transfers must use an applicable lawful mechanism and safeguards where required; ask us for details.

Retention and deletion

We keep information while needed for the service, then only for security, legal duties or disputes; the period depends on data and purpose. Account deletion revokes sessions, removes active memberships and anonymises name and email. Historical loans, cabinet events, support and audit records may remain for accountability or lawful reasons. A cabinet Super Admin must transfer or remove that responsibility first; support can help.

Rights, children and contact

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability and withdraw consent. EEA and UK users may complain to a supervisory authority; US state residents may have additional rights. We may verify identity and apply lawful exceptions. The service is for authorised organisational users, not children under 16. Email ytwl1013@gmail.com; do not send passwords or cabinet codes.
