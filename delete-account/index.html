Delete your account

You can request deletion of your UnicLife Key Cabinet account here even if you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in.

Request deletion from the web

Send a request to ytwl1013@gmail.com, preferably from the email address used for your account. Put “Delete my UnicLife Key Cabinet account” in the subject. If you no longer have access to that mailbox, say so and provide a safe way to contact you. We will verify account ownership before completing deletion. Do not send your password, cabinet access codes or identity documents in your first message.

Email deletion request

A support team member can start and help complete the process without requiring you to reinstall the app.

Delete from the app

While signed in, open Account → Account & security → Delete account and confirm. If you are a Super Admin for a cabinet, transfer that role or delete the cabinet first. If you cannot complete those steps, use the web request above and explain the issue so support can help.

What is deleted and what may remain

Deletion disables access, revokes sessions, removes your active cabinet memberships, clears notification tokens and consent records, and replaces your account email and display name with anonymous values. The app's local profile photo can be removed by deleting the app from the device.

Historical cabinet events, loan references and audit records may remain when necessary for security, accountability, legal obligations or other organization members' rights. Some support conversation records may also need to be retained for these purposes. We will explain any applicable retention in response to your request. See the Privacy Policy's retention section.
