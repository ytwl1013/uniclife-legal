Request deletion online

Email ytwl1013@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my UnicLife Key Cabinet account”, preferably from your account address. If you cannot access that mailbox, tell us how to contact you safely. We will verify ownership and help without requiring you to reinstall the app.

Email deletion request

Delete in the app

While signed in, open Account → Account & security → Delete account and confirm. A cabinet Super Admin must transfer the role or delete the cabinet first. Support can help.

What happens to data

Deletion revokes sessions, removes active memberships and notification tokens, and anonymises account name and email. Historical loans, cabinet events, support and audit records may remain for security, law or other organisation members. We will explain applicable retention when replying.
