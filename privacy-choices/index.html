Make a privacy request

Email ytwl1013@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, if available. State which of the following you want and the email address associated with the account or the organization that entered your information. Please do not send your password, cabinet codes or identity documents in your first message.

Email a privacy request

  • Access or copy: request information we hold about you or, where applicable, a portable copy.
  • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate account or service information. You can also change your login email and display name in the app.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your account and associated data. See the dedicated deletion page.
  • Restriction or objection: ask us to restrict or stop processing where applicable law allows.
  • Consent: withdraw consent for processing that relies on consent, or turn off optional notifications in the app or device settings.

We may verify your request and may need to coordinate with the organization that controls information its administrators entered. We respond within the time required by applicable law; EEA and UK requests are generally answered within one month, subject to permitted extensions. If you are in the EEA or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Advertising, sale and tracking choices

The app has no third-party advertising and does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is therefore no advertising opt-out switch to change on this page. If these practices change, we will update this page and provide any required controls before using information for those purposes.

Other controls

In the app, you can change your email, name and password, choose a profile photo that stays on your device, manage optional device notification permissions, and delete your account. You can revoke camera or photo-library permission in your device settings; scanning and photo selection may stop working. To make a request after uninstalling the app, use the email route above.

Read the Privacy Policy for details about information, service providers, international transfers and retention.
